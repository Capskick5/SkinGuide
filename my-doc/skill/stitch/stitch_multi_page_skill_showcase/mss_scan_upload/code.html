Tips for best results

  • check_circle

    Good Lighting

    Use natural daylight facing a window if possible. Avoid harsh overhead shadows or extreme backlighting.

  • check_circle

    Bare Face

    Remove all makeup, glasses, and gently pull your hair back away from your forehead and cheeks.

  • check_circle

    Neutral Expression

    Keep your face completely relaxed and look directly at the camera lens at eye level.

  • cancel

    Avoid Filters

    Do not use any beauty filters, skin-smoothing effects, or portrait modes that artificially blur details.
